2. Generate Limitations

Activate the existing disabled:

Generate Limitations

functionality.

The purpose is to take the complete authoritative text of a selected monitored independent claim and propose a structured set of claim limitations.

The AI-generated output must initially be:

DRAFT — AI GENERATED

It must never automatically become VERIFIED.

Only an authorized ADMIN or ATTORNEY may verify it.

3. Limitation methodology

The goal is not simply to split the claim at every semicolon.

The analysis should identify technically meaningful limitations suitable for later:

infringement searching;
evidence mapping;
detectability analysis; and
claim-chart construction.

Preserve relationships within the claim where separating language would materially alter its meaning.

For example, the system should distinguish between:

preamble/context;
structural elements;
functional requirements;
relationships between elements;
sequential/process requirements;
wherein clauses;
nested limitations.

Do not rewrite a limitation into broader language than the authoritative claim.

4. Preserve authoritative language

Each generated limitation should retain:

Limitation ID

Exact Claim Language

Normalized/Search Concept

Rationale

Confidence

Uncertainty/Notes

The most important field is Exact Claim Language.

This should consist of the actual claim language associated with the limitation rather than an AI paraphrase.

The AI may separately provide a normalized/search concept, but that must never replace the authoritative language.

5. Example conceptual structure

For a claim beginning:

1. An apparatus, comprising: circuitry for: receiving a first image and a second image; estimating a motion transform...

the resulting structure might conceptually contain:

L01 — Preamble / apparatus

Exact language:
An apparatus, comprising:

L02 — Image reception

Exact language:
circuitry for: receiving a first image and a second image;

L03 — Motion transform

Exact language:
estimating a motion transform between the first image and the second image;

etc.

This is only an illustration of the required structure. Do NOT hard-code this example or assume these are the correct legal limitation boundaries.

7. Claim coverage validation

Add an important validation step before verification.

The system should determine whether the proposed limitation structure collectively accounts for the entire substantive text of the authoritative claim.

Before allowing verification, identify:

Unmapped Claim Language

if any substantive portion of the authoritative claim has not been assigned to a limitation.

Do not silently omit difficult claim language.

The attorney should be able to resolve any unmapped language before verification.

This is particularly important for:

wherein clauses;
nested functional language;
final relational clauses;
unusual punctuation;
Markush language.
