## AI-Enabled Patent Operations

AI hasn't eliminated the need for patent counsel — it has moved the bottleneck upstream. The constraint on portfolio quality is no longer drafting speed; it's the organization's ability to capture, qualify, and prepare inventions in the first place. Engineers don't always know when to disclose. Disclosures arrive inconsistent and incomplete. In-house counsel spend their time rewriting submissions instead of making strategic decisions. And AI tools get introduced without standardized prompting, review, or governance. These are workflow problems before they are legal problems — and we solve them.

Zilka-Kotab serves as an operational extension of the in-house IP department, optimizing the entire patent pipeline from invention conception to issued asset:

**Innovation capture and training.** We train engineers and product teams to recognize patentable subject matter and train inventors to produce complete, AI-ready invention disclosures — so counsel receives decision-ready submissions instead of raw fragments.

**AI workflow design and governance.** We develop standardized disclosure templates, review protocols, and prompting frameworks that let legal departments adopt AI-assisted drafting safely, consistently, and with attorney oversight where it matters.

**Invention review and triage.** We establish and participate in invention review committees, and train managers on the patent-versus-trade-secret decision, so filing dollars flow to the innovations that matter.

**Pipeline insight and executive reporting.** We help IP leadership see and communicate what the pipeline is actually producing — from disclosure quality to portfolio health — with insights that demonstrate value to the business.

**Overflow prosecution and continuity.** As the team maintaining your processes, we are the natural overflow counsel when volume spikes — with none of the ramp-up cost of onboarding a new firm.

**Fractional Chief IP Officer.** For companies too small to justify a full-time IP executive but too sophisticated for purely transactional outside counsel, we provide senior IP leadership on a retained basis: innovation capture, portfolio strategy, prosecution management, international coordination, competitive monitoring, licensing readiness, and board-level reporting.

Engagements are flexibly structured around each client's needs — combining training, invention committee participation, workflow optimization, portfolio health reviews, and overflow prosecution into a system, not billable hours.

## Our Services

**Protect — Building the Portfolio**

Patent preparation and prosecution is the core of our practice: applications built to withstand examination, post-grant challenge, and litigation, filed strategically in the U.S. and worldwide through the PCT and Paris Convention routes with genuine fluency across the USPTO, EPO, and major Asian patent offices. And because not every asset belongs in a patent, we deliver the right protection for each — design patents, trademarks, trade secrets, and copyrights.

**Strategize — Aligning IP with the Business**

We treat the portfolio as a business asset: benchmarking it against competitors, aligning claims with market activity, and matching spend to value. From taking companies at IP "ground zero" to a strategically constructed portfolio, to freedom-to-operate studies, patentability and invalidity opinions, and landscape searching, our strategic work gives leadership a clear-eyed basis for every launch, filing, and risk decision.

**Monetize — Extracting Value**

We have deep experience on both sides of the licensing table — offensive monetization initiatives backed by evidence-of-use analysis and claim charting, and defensive positioning that has preserved royalty-free resolutions against multimillion-dollar demands. We also handle the transactions that move IP — assignments, licenses, covenants not to sue, joint development agreements — and conduct diligence that holds up under investor scrutiny.

**Defend — Protecting the Position**

When patents are challenged or asserted, we bring a prosecution-grounded understanding of how claims are built — and how they break. We represent clients in post-grant proceedings before the PTAB and USPTO, and provide high-leverage litigation support to in-house counsel and trial teams, from invalidity searching to portfolio mining for defensive counterclaims.
