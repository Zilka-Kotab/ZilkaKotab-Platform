I am excited to share the release of BarBlock, my new iOS app blocker.

After trying many screen time and app-blocking tools, I realized that most rely only on software limits, which are easy to ignore, or require you to buy an extra physical device to make unblocking more challenging.

I designed BarBlock to let you block distracting apps by scanning any barcode you already have (e.g., on books and notebooks, cereal boxes, etc.). To unblock your selected apps, you have to scan the same barcode again. That small inconvenience creates a barrier to mindless scrolling. You can also leave your barcode at home (or in any other location) to prevent unblocking until you get back.

BarBlock has no subscriptions, no accounts, and all data stays local on your phone. If you’re interested in productivity or digital wellbeing, I’d love to hear your feedback.
