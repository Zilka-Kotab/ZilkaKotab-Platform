Reduce unnecessary card padding, whitespace, and oversized vertical spacing throughout the expanded pane.

3. Add Estimated Legal Expiration

Determine whether the existing USPTO ODP/file-wrapper data already retrieved by the application provides sufficient information to calculate a reasonable Estimated Legal Expiration.

Use authoritative USPTO data where available, including:

relevant U.S. filing/continuity dates;
application type;
issue date;
Patent Term Adjustment (PTA);
Patent Term Extension (PTE), if available;
terminal disclaimer information, if reliably available;
other USPTO term-related information already accessible.

Do NOT simply calculate expiration from the grant date.

For modern utility patents, implement the applicable statutory-term logic based on the relevant filing/continuity date and applicable PTA/PTE or disclaimer information.

Because not every factor affecting patent term may be reliably obtainable automatically, label the result:

Estimated Legal Expiration

Also provide a small info/tooltip or expandable calculation explanation such as:

Base term date: [date]

PTA: +XXX days

PTE: [if applicable]

Terminal disclaimer: [detected / none detected / unable to determine]

Estimated expiration: [date]

Never fabricate unavailable information.

If the available USPTO records are insufficient to calculate the date reliably, show:

Unable to determine

rather than guessing.

Preserve the source data and provenance used in the calculation.

8. Patent-level monitoring control

In the expanded patent pane, provide an obvious control allowing an authorized ADMIN/ATTORNEY to change the entire patent's monitoring state.

I want this to be more obvious than relying only on the three-dot menu.

Use something like:

Monitoring Status

[● Active Monitoring ▼]

with options:

Active Monitoring
Paused / Not Monitoring

Or use a clean toggle if that is more intuitive.

Avoid confusing terminology. Prefer Active Monitoring / Paused over simply Monitor/Unmonitor if “Unmonitor” would imply that the patent or its history is being removed.

Changing to Paused must:

preserve the patent in the project;
preserve monitored-claim selections;
preserve analysis history;
preserve findings/evidence;
prevent future scheduled monitoring runs from including the patent.

This is separate from Archive/Remove.
