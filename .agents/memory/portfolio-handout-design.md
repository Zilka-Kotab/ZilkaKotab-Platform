name: Portfolio handout design
description: Intent and content boundaries for the public portfolio snapshot PDF.
---

The portfolio download is intentionally a single-page, navy-and-gold snapshot inspired by the supplied Miovision handout. Reserve the bottom two-fifths for the firm's brand and practice copy. Exclude event branding, strategic takeaways/questions, and outside-counsel data from this PDF, without removing counsel from the webpage.

**Why:** The user wants a concise client-facing handout rather than a multipage printout of every on-screen section.

**How to apply:** Keep compact source and sample-size notes with the data. Do not reintroduce advice, event material, or counsel rankings when adding snapshot metrics.
